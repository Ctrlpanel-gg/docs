To accept payments, you need to set up one of the available payment gateways or write an addon for required one yourself.

### PayPal

To accept live payments through PayPal, you need a business account. Personal accounts only support Sandbox payments.

#### Live payments

1. Go to [Paypal Developer Portal](https://developer.paypal.com/dashboard/applications/).
2. Select **Live** in the top left corner.
3. Click **Create App** button.
4. Copy your `Client ID` and `Secret key`.
5. Scroll down to "Webhooks" section.
6. Click **Add Webhook** button.
7. Set webhook URL to `https://your.ctrlpanel.url/payment/PayPalWebhook` and event types to **All Events**, or if you want fine-grained permissions:
    - Checkout -> Checkout order approved
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture completed
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture pending
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture denied
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture declined
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture refunded
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture reversed
8. Copy your `Webhook ID`
9. Paste the copied data into the corresponding fields in the CtrlPanel settings.

#### Sandbox payments

1. Go to [Paypal Developer Portal](https://developer.paypal.com/dashboard/applications/).
2. Select **Sandboox** in the top left corner.
3. Click **Create App** button.
4. Copy your `Client ID` and `Secret key`.
5. Scroll down to "Webhooks" section.
6. Click **Add Webhook** button.
7. Set webhook URL to `https://your.ctrlpanel.url/payment/PayPalWebhook` and event types to **All Events**, or if you want fine-grained permissions:
    - Checkout -> Checkout order approved
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture completed
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture pending
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture denied
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture declined
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture refunded
    - Payments & Payouts -> Payment capture reversed
8. Copy your `Webhook ID`
9. Paste the copied data into the corresponding fields in the CtrlPanel settings.

Now, go to your CtrlPanel root directory (`/var/www/ctrlpanel/`) and open your `.env` file. Find `APP_ENV` variable, and change it from `production` to `local`.  
You can now make test payments using PayPal Sandbox account.

### Stripe

To use Stripe as your payment gateway, you first have to setup a "company" on Stripe's dashboard which you can find [here](https://dashboard.stripe.com/account/onboarding/business-structure)

To setup Stripe, go to the Developer page and click on API Keys. You only need the Secret key in order to use Stripe as your payment gateway. Once you have the secret key go to your Ctrlpanel setup and paste the secret into the `Secret Key` section.

Now, you will need to setup a Stripe Webhook in order to accept payments that are not 100% instantly confirmed by stripe (Klarna, Bank-Transfer etc.).  
To do so, click on Webhooks and add an endpoint.  
The endpoint route would look something like this: `https://your.ctrlpanel.url/payment/StripeWebhooks`. Change the `your.ctrlpanel.url` to your Ctrlpanel setup link.  
Choose the `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.processing` and `payment_intent.payment_failed` events to be sent to your WebhookRoute.  
After the webhook creation, you need to get the endpoint secret and copy paste it to your Ctrlpanel payment settings. The endpoint secret can be found in the Webhook Overview page.

Just like Paypal, you can change it from Live to Sandbox and vice-versa in case you want to test to make sure that your payments are working correctly.

### MercadoPago

This section explains how to get the **Access Token** and **Webhook Secret** you need to integrate Mercado Pago as your payment gateway.

#### Prerequisites

- A Mercado Pago account (active)
- Access to the email linked to your account

#### Step 1 – Access the Developers Portal

1. Go to [https://developers.mercadolibre.com/](https://developers.mercadolibre.com/)
2. Pick your **country** from the dropdown.
3. Log in with your Mercado Pago credentials.


<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">

Make sure you choose the correct country – credentials are **country-specific**.
</Callout>


#### Step 2 – Create a New Application

1. Navigate to [https://www.mercadopago.com/developers/panel/app](https://www.mercadopago.com/developers/panel/app)
2. Click **"Create application"**.
3. Give it a **name** (e.g., your store name).
4. For **Integration type**, select **"Online Payments"**.
5. For **Business model**, choose **"Own development"**.

#### Step 3 – Configure Your Application

1. In **"Your store URL"**, enter the link to your dashboard or website (recommended but optional).
2. Choose **"Checkout API"** as the payment method.
3. Then select **"Payments API"**.


<Callout type="info" title="Info">

Even though the store URL is optional, it helps with tracking and validation.
</Callout>


#### Step 4 – Finalise Creation

1. Accept the terms and conditions.
2. Solve the CAPTCHA.
3. Click **"Continue"**.

#### Step 5 – Activate Production Credentials

1. Inside your new app, go to the **"Production Credentials"** section.
2. Pick your **business sector** – we recommend **"IT Services"** if it fits.
3. Authorise and solve the CAPTCHA again.
4. Click **"Activate credentials"**.


<Callout type="info" title="Success">

Once activated, your **Access Token** and **Public Key** will be shown here.
</Callout>


#### Step 6 – Set Up Your Webhook URL

1. In the same app panel, go to **"Webhooks"**.
2. Click **"Configure notifications"**.
3. Turn on **"Production mode"**.
4. In the **"URL"** field, enter the full webhook URL that Mercado Pago will call. This must be:  
   `https://cpgg.url/payment/MercadoPagoWebhook`  
   (replace `cpgg.url` with your actual CtrlPanel domain).
5. Tick the following events:
   - ✅ **Order (Mercado Pago)**
   - ✅ **Payments (legacy)**
   - ✅ **Plans and Subscriptions**
6. Click **"Save configuration"**.


<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

After saving, you will get your **Webhook Secret** – keep it secure and never expose it publicly.
</Callout>


#### Credentials Summary

| Credential         | Purpose                                           | Where to find it                |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Access Token**   | Authenticate API requests                         | Production Credentials          |
| **Public Key**     | Frontend encryption (only)                        | Production Credentials          |
| **Webhook Secret** | Validate incoming webhook notifications           | Webhook configuration           |

#### Next Steps

- Store all credentials as **environment variables**.
- Make sure your server can receive and process webhooks at the URL you set.
- Read the [official Mercado Pago docs](https://www.mercadopago.com/developers/en/docs) for detailed integration.

### Mollie

_The built-in Mollie settings contain an API key, an enabled flag, and a webhook secret. Configure the API key for the environment you are testing and choose a strong webhook secret. The extension creates checkout requests and sends its callback URL to Mollie as /payment/MollieWebhook with a token query parameter derived from that secret.

The callback checks the token against the configured secret before retrieving/verifying payment state through the Mollie API. Make sure the callback is reachable over HTTPS and that proxy/routing changes preserve its query parameters. Test successful, pending, canceled, and repeated callbacks in staging before enabling the gateway for customers._
